IPC to BNS Finder

All 358 BNS sections are included. Describe what happened in your own words, like "neighbour stole my phone" or "husband demands dowry". It understands synonyms, typos and common Hindi words such as chori, dhokha and dhamki. Section numbers work too.

Please read: this is a quick-reference tool, not an official or legal source. Data comes from the Delhi Police Academy BNS Handbook (Edition II, May 2024) and is cross-checked between its two tables, but errors can remain, and a few pairings are marked CHECK where the handbook contradicts itself. Before filing a complaint, drafting a charge or giving legal advice, confirm the section in the official Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 text (India Code, indiacode.nic.in, or the Gazette of India) or ask a qualified lawyer. Offences committed before 1 July 2024 are still tried under the IPC.
